# Faith Craft Math

A free, offline Bible adventure game for iPad that practices math skills (Pre-K through Grade 8). No ads, no chat, no accounts, no data collection.

- **Play:** https://lizzkz-art.github.io/faith-craft-math/play/
- **About:** https://lizzkz-art.github.io/faith-craft-math/

Open the Play link in Safari, then tap Share > **Add to Home Screen**. It works offline after the first load.

Optional "Follow school order (MCPS)" setting: topics follow the published unit order of the math program Montgomery County Public Schools uses (Amplify Desmos Math), aligned to the Maryland College and Career Ready Standards. Not affiliated with MCPS or Amplify.

Not a medical or therapy product. Bible text: World English Bible (British Edition), public domain. Fonts: Lexend and OpenDyslexic (SIL OFL). 3D engine: three.js (MIT).
