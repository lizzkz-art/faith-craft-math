# Curriculum sources and alignment notes (Faith Craft Math)

Checked on Sunday, Sep 27, 2026 (ET). The app uses only the **order of units and their topic names** from these sources. It doesn't copy any lesson text, problems, or other curriculum materials. Every practice problem in the app is original and generated by the app's own code.

## 1. Which program MCPS uses (confirmed on official MCPS pages)
- **MCPS Elementary Mathematics page:** https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/math/es/
  - Says that starting in 2026–27, MCPS elementary schools (PreK–Grade 5) use **Amplify Desmos Math**, aligned to the Maryland College and Career Ready Standards (MCCRS).
  - Links to the Amplify Caregiver Hub for families.
- **MCPS elementary math update:** https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/math/elementary/update/
  - Says a Grade 4 cluster-grouping model also begins in 2026–27.
- **Board of Education update, May 1, 2026:** https://ww2.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/departments/publicinfo/community/school-year-2025-2026/BOE-Update-20260501.html
  - The Board adopted Amplify Desmos Math for PreK through Integrated Algebra 2 on Apr 30, 2026.
- **Adopted Board memo (PDF):** https://ww2.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/departments/procurement/uploads/4983.1/11.01%20ADOPTED%20Math%20Curriculum%20Approval%20260430%20(1).pdf
  - Before this, MCPS used Eureka Math (elementary) and Illustrative Mathematics (middle school).

## 2. Unit order and unit lengths (publisher scope and sequence)
- **Amplify Desmos Math K–5 scope and sequence (PDF):** https://rebuild.prod.amplify.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/K-5-ScopeSequence.pdf
  - Source for unit titles, order, and suggested days per unit.
- **Amplify Caregiver Hub, Amplify Desmos Math K–5:** https://amplify.com/caregiver-hub/amplify-desmos-math-k-5/
  - Used to cross-check unit titles.
- **Amplify Desmos Math 6–A1 program guide (PDF):** https://rebuild.prod.amplify.com/pdf/uploads/2024/07/ADM_Sampling2024_6%E2%80%93A1-ProgramGuide_MA0477_071224_v1.pdf
  - Source for the Grade 6–8 unit order and days.
- **PreK:** unit titles only. No day counts were found, so the app spaces PreK units evenly (about 20 days each). This is an approximation.

### Grade 4 order used in the app (days = publisher's suggested instructional days)
1. Factors and Multiples (15)
2. Fraction Equivalence and Comparison (21)
3. Extending Operations to Fractions (21)
4. From Hundredths to Hundred Thousands (26)
5. Multiplicative Comparison and Measurement (21)
6. Multiplying and Dividing Multi-Digit Numbers (25)
7. Angles and Properties of Shapes (24)

The Grade 3 review draws on Grade 3 Units 1–7: multiplication, area, adding and subtracting within 1,000, division, fractions as numbers, measurement and time, and shapes and perimeter.

## 3. School calendar (for "what unit is it now")
- **MCPS 2026–27 calendar announcement:** https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/news/mcps-news/2025/12/school-year-calendar/
  - Dates used: first day Aug 25, 2026; last day Jun 16, 2027; Labor Day Sep 7; winter break Dec 23–Jan 3; spring break Mar 26–Apr 4.
  - Other single-day closures weren't verified, so the app counts about 197 weekdays instead of the official ~180. This makes the date estimate slightly slow late in the year.

## 4. Maryland standards
- **MSDE revised MCCRS for Mathematics** (adopted Jul 29, 2025; based on Common Core): https://marylandpublicschools.org/about/pages/dcaa/math/revised-standards.aspx
- **Grade 4 MCCRS Math Standard Companion Guide (PDF):** https://marylandpublicschools.org/about/Documents/DCAA/Math/revised/Grade-4-MCCRS-Math-Standard-Companion-Guide-A.pdf
- Grade 4 fraction work uses denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 100, following the Grade 4 standard (4.NF). The app's Grade 4 fraction problems stick to these.

## 5. What is confirmed vs. estimated
- **Confirmed:** MCPS uses Amplify Desmos Math for PreK–5 in 2026–27 and adopted it for middle school too. The unit titles and order come from Amplify's published scope and sequence.
- **Not found:** an MCPS-specific 2026–27 pacing calendar (which week each unit starts). **The current unit is estimated** by spreading the publisher's unit days across the school calendar.
  - On Sep 27, 2026, this puts Grade 4 in Unit 2 (Fraction Equivalence and Comparison).
  - Parents can set the exact current unit in Parent area → Settings → Current unit.
- **My own work, needs review:** the matching of each app skill to a unit (in `js/math/curriculum.js`) and the plain-words topic names. It isn't an official MCPS or Amplify crosswalk.
- **Fallback:** if MCPS changes its program, or a parent turns off "Follow school order (MCPS)", the app follows each skill's level. That order follows the Maryland/Common Core grade-level standards.
